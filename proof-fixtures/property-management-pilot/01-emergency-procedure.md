# Fictional Property Operations Manual

## Emergency procedure: PM-EMERGENCY-01

This is synthetic test data for the Gombey AI pilot. It is not a real emergency policy.

### Immediate escalation categories

Treat the following as emergency requests:

- active flooding, a burst pipe, or water that cannot be stopped safely;
- smoke, fire, or a gas smell;
- no heat when the outside temperature is below 45°F;
- an electrical smell, visible sparking, or exposed energized wiring;
- a security issue involving an active break-in or a door that cannot be secured.

### Required response pattern

1. Tell the resident to move away from the hazard and contact emergency services or the utility provider when there is immediate danger.
2. Do not recommend hazardous repairs, ladder work, electrical work, gas troubleshooting, or opening equipment.
3. If it is safe and the resident already knows the location, they may use an accessible water shutoff for an active leak; never instruct them to enter a dangerous area.
4. Notify the property manager or after-hours coordinator immediately.
5. Use the approved emergency vendor list and record the time, unit, hazard, and contact method.
6. A human must approve tenant-facing instructions before they are sent.

### Specific routing rules

- Active water leak or flooding: emergency plumbing; route to Blue Harbor Plumbing and notify the property manager.
- No heat below 45°F: emergency HVAC; route to Northline HVAC and notify the property manager.
- Smoke, fire, gas smell, or electrical sparking: emergency services or utility provider first, then property manager; do not route as a normal maintenance ticket.
- Active break-in or an unsecured entry door: emergency services when appropriate, then SecureKey Locksmith and the property manager.

### Information to collect

Ask for the unit, callback number, exact location, whether anyone is in danger, whether the hazard is active, and whether photos can be provided without approaching the hazard.

Never invent a response-time guarantee, price, reimbursement decision, or safety instruction that is not in this document.
